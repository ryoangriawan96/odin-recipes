Turmeric Tamarind Elixir

Description

A classic Indonesian traditional healing drink, also known as Kunyit Asam. It is famous for its anti-inflammatory properties and refreshing sweet-sour taste.

Ingredients

  • 100g fresh turmeric roots (peeled and grated)
  • 50g tamarind paste
  • 100g palm sugar
  • 4 cups of water

Steps

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a medium pot.
  2. Add the grated turmeric, tamarind paste, and palm sugar.
  3. Simmer on low heat for 15 minutes.
  4. Strain the liquid into a glass and serve chilled.
