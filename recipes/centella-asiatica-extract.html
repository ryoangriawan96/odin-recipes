Centella Asiatica Extract

Description

Commonly known as Gotu Kola or Pegagan this herb is widely used for enhancing brain function and promoting healthy skin.

Ingredients

  • 1 handful of fresh Centella asiatica leaves
  • 1 cup of warm water
  • 1 tablespoon of honey

Steps

  1. Wash the Centella leaves thoroughly.
  2. Blend the leaves with warm water until smooth.
  3. Strain the extract using a fine cloth or strainer.
  4. Stir in the honey and drink immediately.
