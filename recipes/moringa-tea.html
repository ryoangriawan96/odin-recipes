Moring Oleifera Herbal Tea

Description

This Moringa oleifera herbal tea is a highly nutritious beverage. Often referred to as a miracle plant, it is packed with antioxidants and provides a powerful natural energy boost without caffeine.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons of dried Moringa leaves
  • 2 cups of hot water
  • 1 teaspoon of raw honey (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a small pot or kettle.
  2. Add the dried Moringa leaves to a teapot and pour the hot water over them.
  3. Let the mixture steep for 5 minutes.
  4. Strain the tea into a cup, stir in the honey, and serve warm.
